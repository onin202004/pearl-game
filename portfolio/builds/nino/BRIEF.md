2. **Journey** (authored, from his own old site order, re-sequenced for conversion):

4. **Feeling, stage by stage, and the one moment**: see feeling curve and peak.
5. **What no other site does** (authored): the page is cut like the ads he sells.
   A real editing timeline runs along the bottom, scroll is the playhead, and the

6. **Range**: away from premium-minimal, toward loud and dense. Safety yellow
   as a full ground, not a sprinkle.
7. **One world or distinct scenes**: distinct scenes, hard cuts. An editor's page
   should be made of cuts.
8. **Assets**: his own footage only. No generated imagery, no spend.

## Landing-page psychology applied (authored)

- **Five-second test.** Above the fold: who he helps, the outcome, one action.
  Four text elements max.
- **Self-selection.** Name the buyer (DTC brands, high-ticket coaches) so the right
  visitor thinks "this is for me" and agencies self-filter, per his answer.
- **Show, do not claim.** Work starts on the second screen, autoplaying muted.
  Real brand names (Freebird, Spudsy, Kamado Range, Visus, BloomChic) as proof.
- **One action, one label.** "Book a call" everywhere, same link.
- **Social proof near the ask.** Clients on camera right before About and the close.
- **Specific over clever.** No invented numbers. The only figure is his real
  "5 years".
- **Peak-end rule.** The best moment is engineered near the end, then the ask.
- **Low friction contact.** Call, WhatsApp, email all one tap, numbers that match.

## Feeling curve

1. Hook: *"oh, this guy is sharp"*. Name at poster scale, his cutout in front of a phone playing his own ad.
2. Problem: *a small sting of recognition*. Three short hard-cut lines about ads that get skipped.
3. Brand proof: *impressed*. Four product ads playing at once like a shelf.
4. Coach proof: *"this could be my ad"*. Founder-led ads, the coach buyer sees himself.
5. Visus: *delight*. One product, four stories, cut fast.
6. AI ads: *curiosity*. Ads made with AI, labelled honestly.
7. Clients: *trust*. Real people on camera.
8. About: *connection*. The only quiet section. Space, a photo, plain words.
9. Peak: *the click*. The timeline expands: "You just watched a 30-second ad."
10. Close: *decision*. Yellow ground, one button.

## The peak

"The whole site was edited like an ad, and the timeline at the bottom shows it."
Lives in the fixed chrome (the timeline), triggered on the last act. Biggest
visual change on the page: the 44px bar rises into a full panel.

## Tell-someone sentence

"It's the site where you realise at the end that you just scrolled through one of his ads."

## Authored silence

The About section (act 8) is deliberately calm: no autoplay, no motion beyond a
single reveal. It is the pause before the peak, not dead scroll.
